01 · Start here

Three things to know before anything else

The rest of this page is reference material. These three points are the ones that make it worth reading.

Where the value is

Companies that haven't raised equity yet

Grant, licensing and spinout signals are tracked on Dealroom before a priced round appears. That's how the trial surfaced ~50 names that fit the thesis and weren't on the radar, and it's the gap most equity-first databases leave open.

How to work it

MCP and the API first, platform for alerts

Query Dealroom from Claude or Perplexity, or script it against the API. Use the platform for the things it does well: saved-search alerts, lists and exports. Four searches and six curated landscapes are already set up for you.

When something's missing

Send it over

Enrichment of thin profiles, finer classification of a thesis area and bespoke landscapes are part of the relationship, not extras to negotiate. The faster we hear about a gap, the faster it closes.

03 · Searching a niche thesis

Tags, keywords and the filters that do the work

You asked why the searches use tags rather than industries or technologies. The short answer is granularity. The longer answer is below.

FilterUse it whenExample for ECV
TagsYou want something niche. Tags are the most granular classification and are what the saved searches use.methane detection, renewable natural gas, carbon capture, fuel cells, rare earth elements
KeywordsThe concept is newer than the taxonomy, or you want to match on how a company describes itself."data center cooling", "biogas upgrading", "immersion cooling", "critical minerals recovery"
IndustriesBroad sweeps and market sizing, not sourcing.Energy, Deep tech
TechnologiesCross-cutting enablers rather than end markets.Quantum, Advanced materials
Funding signalsCatching companies before the equity round.Grant received, university spinout, total raised under $5M, no Series A
Founded / HQKeeping the set fresh and in the geographies you cover.Founded 2020+ (2024+ for the youngest cut); HQ US, Canada or Europe

Habits that work

  • Start wide with tags OR'd across a thesis area, then layer funding and founded-date filters. Too narrow too early is how good companies disappear.
  • Save your own version of every search and switch on alerts for new entrants.
  • Export to CSV when you want the set in your own tracker.

Habits that waste credits

  • Pointing the AI agent at 200k+ companies with no filters. Scope it to a saved search of 150–250 first, then ask it to rank or enrich.
  • Searching by industry alone for a niche like methane detection; it returns the whole energy sector.
  • Re-running a broad search weekly by hand when an alert on a saved search does it for you.

05 · Methodology and optional extras

Why companies surface earlier, and what's optional

How the data is built

Dealroom tracks non-dilutive and pre-equity events: public grants, university licensing and spinout registrations, accelerator cohorts and founder moves. Most databases index a company when its first priced round is announced; by then the round is competitive.

Companies carry tags, technologies and industries rather than one category, so a methane-detection sensor company is findable as methane detection, as oil & gas services and as sensors. Automated collection is layered with a research team that maintains the landscapes, verifies rounds and resolves duplicates.

Stealth companies aren't there until they leave a trace, and some niches don't have a dedicated tag yet, which is what keywords are for. If a company you care about is thin on data, tell us; we enrich on request, and any thesis area can be classified more finely with the insights team.
